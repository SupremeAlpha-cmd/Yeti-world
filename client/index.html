How it works

A survival arena with real stakes. No luck, no grinding — just reflexes.

1
Connect walletLink any wallet on Robinhood Chain. You'll need USDG for the entry fee plus a little ETH for gas.
2
Pay entryWhen your room fills, approve the USDG entry onchain. Every player's entry goes into one prize pot — the smart contract holds it, not us.
3
SurvivePick your animal — bull, bear, ape, whale, wolf, or frog — and dodge the memecoins raining down. $RUG, $HONEYPOT, $DUMPIT… one touch and you're out.
4
Get paidLast animal standing wins. The contract sends 95% of the pot straight to the winner's wallet, plus a YETI bonus. 5% goes to the treasury to keep the arena running.
